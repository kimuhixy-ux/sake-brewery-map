Purpose of this site

Japan Sake Brewery Map is an independently run PWA for finding sake breweries, wineries, and craft beer breweries across Japan on a single map, for learning and browsing purposes. Sake, shochu, awamori, craft beer, and wine are distinguished by marker shape, with notes on featured brands and award history. Data is compiled from OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) and public sources such as the Japan Sake and Shochu Makers Association.
